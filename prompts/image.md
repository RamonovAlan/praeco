# Image production instruction

Version: 0.3 premium minimalist editorial

Create exactly one original 1536x1024 landscape image from the approved private brief and the current generation request.

Translate the post's single central tension into one clear visual metaphor. The image must reinforce the argument rather than illustrate the text literally, and it must work without embedded text.

Use a premium minimalist 3D editorial aesthetic with a conceptual cinematic still-life quality:

- one dominant subject and no more than two supporting objects;
- generous negative space, low visual clutter, a clear focal point, and strong depth;
- balanced composition that stays legible in a mobile professional feed;
- realistic tactile surfaces such as matte stone, brushed metal, ceramic, or frosted glass;
- soft cinematic or warm directional light, natural shadows, controlled reflections, and subtle glow only when meaningful;
- a restrained base of slate, warm stone, and soft neutral tones;
- sparse muted amber, jade, plum-noir, or cool-blue accents only when they support the idea.

Choose the scene from the post's tension. Architectural, industrial, or science-fiction elements are optional and must be conceptually necessary. Prefer symbolic clarity over spectacle. Keep the composition original for every post while preserving a recognizable visual identity.

Do not name or imitate a living artist, copy a reference composition, reproduce recognizable characters, or insert third-party logos. Do not generate real colleagues, clients, public figures, or private locations. Avoid generic office stock imagery, generic technology clichés, decorative robots, default cyberpunk, excessive neon, unrelated symbols, unnecessary objects, visual clickbait, unreadable text, signatures, and watermarks.

Use the approved private brief as the source of truth. A revision request may change the previous image only as explicitly requested. Do not add unsupported people, events, claims, or other factual details.

Return only the image. Do not return planning text and do not create variants.
